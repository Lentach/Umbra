## What was done
- **Owner decision 141 (C2):** opt-in GIF search, chosen over a backend proxy (our server would see every user's searches) and over removing the picker.
- `SettingsProvider.gifSearch` (prefs `gif_search`): device-local, OFF by default, the `receiptsAndTyping` pattern.
- `GifPickerSheet.show` asks first while it is off: a GlassDialog names Giphy, the searches, the previews, the picked GIF and the IP. Cancel, an outside tap or back is a no.
  - Every Giphy request starts in the picker: search, previews, and `sendGif`'s download of the picked GIF. So nothing reaches Giphy before a yes. Receivers never contacted Giphy (box media).
- Settings → Privacy & Safety: a "Wyszukiwanie GIF-ów (Giphy)" switch (`privacy-gif-search-row`).
- Existing users lose GIF search until they say yes once (intended: opt-in).
- Docs: plan row C2 ✅, METADATA (out of "known gaps", own paragraph), decision 141, `frontend/CLAUDE.md` §3 (never call `sendGif` with a Giphy URL from elsewhere), README.
